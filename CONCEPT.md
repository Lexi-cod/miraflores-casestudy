# Miraflores: review the claim, follow the evidence

**Click any amount to see the source page, calculation and review conclusion.** The workpaper is an evidence graph with an assigned review process. Preparers work through open items; experienced reviewers retain control over adjustments, judgments and sign-off. This reduces the navigation and repeated questions that consume review time.

The supplied packet stays intact: 74 documents, 3,012 extracted values and three computed returns. A high extraction confidence never counts as verification. Opening a form shows clickable amounts; large schedules retain a searchable detail view. The evidence drawer keeps the amount in context, opens the cited page, highlights reliably matched text and exposes supporting records only when needed. Reverse tracing follows a source through direct and downstream return use.

| Record | Identity and purpose |
| --- | --- |
| Source | Document/page, extracted field, client answer, message, attachment, prior-year or agency reference; original text and attribution |
| Claim | Version + form/line key; engine amount, inputs, computation rule, recorded derivation and related records |
| Correction | Separate working amount or missing detail; original preserved, author, reason, evidence and packet-rate estimate |
| Review | Verification method, conclusion, reviewer, value/version, notes, judgments with authority and completeness decisions |
| Responsibility | Engagement team and inherited form ownership, delegated tasks, deadlines and preparation/review handoffs |
| Authorization | Independent adjustment approvals, ordered signatures and spouse authorizations bound to a package fingerprint |
| History | Transactional state, revisions, attributed hash-linked events, view logs, frozen snapshots, amendments and next-year source history |

A correction reopens affected tie-outs and signatures. Independent adjustment review records whether its estimated effect belongs in the total; material effects also require partner approval. The supplied rates estimate tax changes without inventing an engine rerun. Reviewers must resolve duplicated downstream effects. Filing readiness checks forms, documents, open notes/findings, judgments, outside filings, verification, signatures and authorizations.

Roles control server responses as well as buttons. Administrators allocate access without seeing the tax packet; clients see their own questions and authorization. Separate tab sessions and revision checks support concurrent review. A portable HTML binder retains the return, evidence and decisions for offline examination; next-year intake carries source history and assigned follow-up work.

The app makes no external AI calls. Local mode saves to SQLite; the free Render submission uses encrypted saved state in Postgres. Free database expiry and case-study boundaries are documented in COMPLIANCE.md and DEPLOYMENT.md.

**AI disclosure:** Codex assisted with requirements review, design, implementation, packet-specific checks and documentation. Earlier revisions had automated/browser checks; this update used only source inspection and syntax validation at the user's request. Professional conclusions remain attributed human decisions, never pre-approved by the tool.
